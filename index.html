§ 02 · Abstract

Building the future of AI

I'm a researcher and architect focused on pushing the boundaries of neural architectures and sequence modeling.

With over a decade of experience in software development, system architecture, and AI research, I've led projects from early-stage crypto systems to enterprise-scale solutions.

My current work explores modular architectures, speculative execution for latency hiding, and post-transformer paradigms for efficient AI systems — documented openly in the papers and notes on this site.

I also serve as COO of Levier IA, where I lead technical operations and AI product architecture: the Levier-Learn™ platform, a suite of AI diagnostics, and autonomous agent infrastructure on the edge.

Correspondence: romainabdelaal@gmail.com · Current affiliation: COO, Levier IA · Research interests: modular architectures, vTRN sequence models, speculative decoding.
