Lessons from Building AstralAGI

In 2024 I built a specialized AI agent for generative astrology — partly as a product, mostly as a controlled experiment in domain-specific AI. Build log and post-mortem, from the timeline entry [Fig. 3].

AstralAGI does one strange thing: it generates astrological interpretations. I chose the domain deliberately. Astrology has a large, stable, self-contained corpus, no ground truth to collide with, and users who immediately notice when an interpretation feels generic. That makes it a perfect testbed for the question I actually cared about: how far can you push a general LLM into being a convincing specialist?

1. Hybrid RAG beats fine-tuning for niche knowledge

The core engine is a hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation system over a vector database populated with a custom corpus of astrological texts. Fine-tuning bakes knowledge into weights — expensive to update, easy to corrupt. Retrieval keeps the corpus editable and auditable: when I refine an interpretation rule, I update a document, not a checkpoint. The fine-tuned parts of the system are reserved for style and voice, not facts.

2. Prompt chaining is an architecture, not a hack

Single-prompt answers plateau fast. AstralAGI chains prompts — chart extraction, aspect detection, thematic synthesis, then interpretation — with tree-of-thought branching so multiple reading candidates get explored and the strongest survives. Done properly, the chain is a reasoning architecture you can debug node by node, the same way I would debug any other pipeline.

3. The boring stack wins

OpenAI and Anthropic APIs, LangChain, Python, vector DBs. Nothing exotic. The differentiator was corpus curation and chain design, not infrastructure. The fastest path to a specialist agent is a general model, a great retrieval layer, and ruthless editing of what you feed it.

4. The lesson that carried into the research

AstralAGI is a small monolith of specialists. The SSR research program is what happens when you take that observation seriously at the architecture level: break the black-box model into modular experts, give each a direct signal, and let them run in parallel. The agent was the hypothesis; the papers are the theory.
